Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

HomeCook ("we", "our", or "the app") is a meal planning application. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Account information: Your name and email address, provided directly or via Google or Facebook sign-in.
  • App data: Recipes you create, meal plans you schedule, shopping lists generated from your plans, and household membership.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, health data, financial information, or device identifiers for tracking purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To store your recipes, meal plans, and shopping lists
  • To enable household sharing (shared calendars and shopping lists with members you invite)
  • To authenticate you via Google, Facebook, or email/password

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase, which uses Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • Google Sign-In: Optional authentication (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Facebook Login: Optional authentication (governed by Meta's Privacy Policy)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Household Sharing

When you join a household via an invite code, other household members can see:

  • Your name and membership status
  • Meal plans added to the shared calendar
  • The shared shopping list

Your personal recipe bookmarks remain private and are not visible to other household members.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data

Children's Privacy

HomeCook is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or by email.
