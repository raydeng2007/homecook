Delete Your Account

You can delete your HomeCook account and all associated data at any time. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the HomeCook app
  2. Go to the Household tab (bottom right)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile — name, email address, and login credentials
  • Your recipes — any recipes you personally created
  • Your bookmarks — saved recipes from the public cookbook
  • Your meal plans — meals you added to the calendar
  • Your household membership — you will be removed from any shared household

This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered. If you are the sole owner of a household, the household and all its shared data will also be deleted.

Alternative: Email Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing hello@rayray.io from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 days.

Facebook Login Users

If you signed up using Facebook Login, deleting your HomeCook account will also remove all data we received from Facebook. You can also manage your Facebook app connections at Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.
